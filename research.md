Nonlinear partial differential equations, regularity versus singularity regimes, hydrodynamics.
{: .page-intro}

My scientific interests revolve around well-posedness questions of partial differential equations
stemming from natural and life sciences, including incompressible and compressible Navier-Stokes
equations, non-Newtonian flows, the related toy models, and chemotaxis.

## Anomalous dissipation and turbulent transport

*with L. Székelyhidi Jr. and B. Wu*

The zeroth law of turbulence asserts that dissipation survives in the vanishing-diffusivity limit.
For scalars advected by a rough velocity field, we established this by a new connection between
homogenisation and the geometric, convex-integration perspective on PDEs.

- *Anomalous dissipation and Euler flows* — the weak zeroth law of scalar turbulence, with the
  velocity governed by an Euler flow. *JEMS, to appear*{: .venue}
- *Scalar anomalous dissipation and optimal regularity via iterated homogenization* — the strong
  zeroth law, beyond the Yaglom threshold, for divergence-free velocity.
  *[arXiv:2604.13912](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13912), submitted*{: .venue}

## Pathological solutions in dissipative fluid models

Dissipation is often expected to rule out wild behaviour. In a series of papers I show that this
expectation fails in natural dissipative settings — including regimes previously believed to be
safely well-posed — by constructing non-unique or otherwise pathological solutions.

- *Non-uniqueness of power-law flows* (with S. Modena, L. Székelyhidi Jr.) — ill-posedness driven
  by the quadratic nonlinearity, for small *p*. *CMP 2021*{: .venue}
- *Non-unique power-law flows with any p ≥ 2* (with S. Schiffer) — ill-posedness driven by
  quasilinearity, now for every *p* ≥ 2. *submitted*{: .venue}
- *Pathological solutions of Navier–Stokes equations on T² with gradients in Hardy spaces* (with
  A. Hidalgo) — very weak solutions in 2d, with the full gradient controlled.
  *JFA, to appear*{: .venue}

## Fractional Keller–Segel systems

*with R. Granero-Belinchón*

Chemotaxis models with fractional dissipation sit exactly on the regularity–blowup threshold,
which makes them a sharp test case for critical and supercritical techniques.

- *Critical Keller–Segel meets Burgers on S¹: large-time smooth solutions* — disproof of the
  finite-time blowup conjecture in the critical one-dimensional case.
  *Nonlinearity 2016*{: .venue}
- *Global solutions for a supercritical drift-diffusion equation* — regularity in certain
  supercritical cases. *Adv. Math. 2016*{: .venue}
- Related: suppression of blowup by a logistic source in the 2d fractional system.
  *JDE 2017*{: .venue}

## Well-posedness beyond duality pairing

*with M. Bulíček and S. Schwarzacher*

For nonlinear systems under singular forcing, the natural energy pairing breaks down. We developed
a well-posedness theory that operates outside it, in the steady and then in the evolutionary
setting.

- *A unified theory for some non-Newtonian fluids under singular forcing*. *SIMA 2016*{: .venue}
- *Well-posedness of nonlinear parabolic systems beyond duality*. *AIHP-C 2019*{: .venue}
