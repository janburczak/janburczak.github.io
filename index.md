Welcome to my webpage! I am a mathematician specializing in partial differential equations.

In spring 2019 I joined Mathematisches Institut, Universität Leipzig as a researcher, after a
two-year postdoctoral stay at OxPDE, Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford. I obtained my
PhD in 2015 from Institute of Mathematics, Polish Academy of Sciences.

My scientific interests revolve around well-posedness questions of partial differential equations
stemming from natural and life sciences, including incompressible and compressible Navier-Stokes
equations, non-Newtonian flows, the related toy models, and chemotaxis.

## Selected results

### Scalar anomalous dissipation

*with L. Székelyhidi Jr. and B. Wu*

A new connection between homogenisation and the geometric (convex-integration) perspective on
PDEs, used to settle the zeroth law of scalar turbulence.

- The weak zeroth law, with velocity governed by an Euler flow. *JEMS, to appear*{: .venue}
- The strong zeroth law, beyond the Yaglom threshold, with divergence-free velocity and optimal
  regularity. *submitted*{: .venue}

### Pathological solutions in dissipative fluid models

- Ill-posedness of power-law models via quadratic nonlinearity, for small *p* (with S. Modena,
  L. Székelyhidi Jr.). *CMP 2021*{: .venue}
- Ill-posedness of power-law models via quasilinearity, for every *p* \not 2 (with S. Schiffer).
  *submitted*{: .venue}
- Very weak solutions of the 2d Navier–Stokes equations with full gradient controlled (with
  A. Hidalgo). *JFA, to appear*{: .venue}

### Regularity for fractional Keller–Segel systems

*with R. Granero-Belinchón*

- Disproof of the finite-time blowup conjecture in the critical 1d case.
  *Nonlinearity 2016*{: .venue}
- Regularity in certain supercritical cases. *Adv. Math. 2016*{: .venue}

### Well-posedness beyond duality pairing

*with M. Bulíček and S. Schwarzacher*

- Steady case: a unified theory for some non-Newtonian fluids under singular forcing.
  *SIMA 2016*{: .venue}
- Evolutionary case: nonlinear parabolic systems beyond duality. *AIHP-C 2019*{: .venue}
