Jan Burczak

Lecturer, Faculty of Mathematics, Leipzig University
Visiting Researcher, Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences

I work on nonlinear partial differential equations, in the regime where regularity and singularity compete: hydrodynamics, turbulent transport, non-Newtonian flows and chemotaxis. Much of my recent work builds bridges between homogenisation and the geometric, convex-integration perspective on PDEs.

Selected results

Scalar anomalous dissipation

with L. Székelyhidi Jr. and B. Wu

A new connection between homogenisation and the geometric (convex-integration) perspective on PDEs, used to settle the zeroth law of scalar turbulence.

  • The weak zeroth law, with velocity governed by an Euler flow. JEMS, to appear
  • The strong zeroth law, beyond the Yaglom threshold, with divergence-free velocity and optimal regularity. submitted

Pathological solutions in dissipative fluid models

  • Ill-posedness of power-law models via quadratic nonlinearity, for small p (with S. Modena, L. Székelyhidi Jr.). CMP 2021
  • Ill-posedness of power-law models via quasilinearity, for every p ≥ 2 (with S. Schiffer). submitted
  • Very weak solutions of the 2d Navier–Stokes equations with full gradient controlled (with A. Hidalgo). JFA, to appear

Regularity for fractional Keller–Segel systems

with R. Granero-Belinchón

  • Disproof of the finite-time blowup conjecture in the critical 1d case. Nonlinearity 2016
  • Regularity in certain supercritical cases. Adv. Math. 2016

Well-posedness beyond duality pairing

with M. Bulíček and S. Schwarzacher

  • Steady case: a unified theory for some non-Newtonian fluids under singular forcing. SIMA 2016
  • Evolutionary case: nonlinear parabolic systems beyond duality. AIHP-C 2019
